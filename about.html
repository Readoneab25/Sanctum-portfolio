Learned from trainers, Self-taught, and built by real projects

I didn't learn to code for the fun of it — I learned by taking on things people actually needed built, and figuring out what it took to finish them properly.

How I work

I'm a front-end developer and self-taught app developer, building for PC, Android, and iPhone. My front end work is HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with React for anything that needs to be more dynamic.

What I care about most is whether something actually works for the person using it day to day — a clinic front desk that needs to trust its backups, a student who needs a dictionary term fast, a business owner who just needs a working website. I've learned equally from finished projects and from the parts that stayed in progress — like waiting on a client for hosting and payment — because that's the real, non-tutorial version of shipping software.
